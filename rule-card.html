  1. Start at the marked point on each side. B starts pointing up (the arrow).
  2. Roll once. Draw one grid step on A, then one grid step on B. Same roll for both.
  3. Repeat 40 times. Always start where the last step ended.
  4. A: look at the rose, three fixed directions. B: look at your last step, add the angle, draw along the grid.
  5. Same die, same pairs of faces, same three options on both sides. A’s random number is the direction; B’s is the change of direction.
  6. If a line reaches the edge, treat that roll as straight.
Rule A: three directions, 120° apart, on the grid lines.
